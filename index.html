Keyboard: Space/Up to jump, X/Right arrow to throw. Tap the left/right halves of the game, or use the buttons. Hop the dogs and throw papers at the orange doorsteps as you pass. You can JUMP and THROW together to do both at once. Jump up to grab the glowing pickups: a bone distracts the dogs, boots give a double jump, the newspaper throws for you, the shield survives one bite, and the heart wins back a lost paper. The tall BONUS office is optional: tap THROW twice while it passes for +50. Falling short costs no strike.
